Свята в США (holidays/usa-holidays.html)

1. New Year's Day

1. Match the words

  • midnight — c північ
  • fireworks — d феєрверк
  • Eve — e переддень свята
  • shout — a кричати
  • promise — b обіцянка

2. Fill in the gaps

  1. December 31st is New Year's Eve.
  2. The clock strikes twelve at midnight.
  3. My resolution is to read more books.
  4. We watch colourful fireworks in the sky.

3. True or False?

  1. New Year's Day is on December 31st. — F
  2. In New York, a big ball drops in Times Square. — T
  3. At midnight people shout "Merry Christmas!" — F
  4. A resolution is a promise to do something better. — T

4. Answer the questions

  1. When does the party start? — On New Year's Eve (the night before).
  2. How many people watch the ball drop? — About a million people.
  3. What is your New Year's resolution? — власна відповідь

2. Presidents' Day

1. Match the words

  • remember — c вшановувати, пам'ятати
  • president — e президент
  • bill — a банкнота
  • sale — b розпродаж
  • birthday — d день народження

2. Who is it? (W / L)

  1. He was the first president. — W
  2. He was the 16th president. — L
  3. He is on the five-dollar bill. — L
  4. His birthday is on February 22nd. — W
  5. He helped to end slavery. — L

3. Choose the right answer

  1. Presidents' Day is in … — b) February
  2. It is always on a … — a) Monday
  3. Washington is on the … bill. — a) one-dollar

4. Answer the questions

  1. Who was the first president of the USA? — George Washington.
  2. What do shops have on Presidents' Day? — Big sales.
  3. Who is the president of Ukraine? — власна відповідь

3. St. Patrick's Day

1. Match the words

  • wear — d носити (одяг)
  • shamrock — e трилисник
  • parade — a парад
  • leaf — b листок
  • pinch — c щипати

2. Odd one out

  1. green, hat, shamrock, turkey — turkey
  2. parade, music, chimney, dancing — chimney
  3. Ireland, Italy, Irish, Patrick — Italy
  4. March, leaf, plant, shamrock — March

3. True or False?

  1. St. Patrick's Day is on March 17th. — T
  2. It is a Mexican holiday. — F
  3. A shamrock has four leaves. — F
  4. In Chicago, people make the river green. — T

4. Answer the questions

  1. What colour do people wear? — Green.
  2. What can happen if you don't wear green? — Someone can pinch you.
  3. What is the symbol of the holiday? — The shamrock.

4. Columbus Day

1. Match the words

  • sailor — d моряк
  • ship — c корабель
  • island — a острів
  • ocean — e океан
  • find — b знайти

2. Numbers from the text

  1. Columbus sailed in 1492.
  2. He had three ships.
  3. Columbus Day is on the second Monday in October.
  4. The first ship was called the Niña.

3. Put the sentences in order

  1. Columbus wanted to find a new way to India.
  2. He sailed across the Atlantic Ocean with three ships.
  3. He found the islands of America.
  4. Today we remember him on Columbus Day.

На аркуші (згори вниз): 3, 1, 4, 2

4. Answer the questions

  1. Where was Columbus from? — From Italy.
  2. Where did he want to go? — To India.
  3. Who lived in America before Columbus came? — Native Americans.

5. Thanksgiving

1. Match the words

  • turkey — c індичка
  • share — a ділитися
  • pumpkin — e гарбуз
  • corn — b кукурудза
  • dessert — d десерт

2. Thanksgiving dinner (їжа з тексту)

turkey, potatoes, corn, cranberry sauce, pumpkin pie (не з тексту: pizza, cookies, ice cream, sushi, hot dogs)

3. Choose the right answer

  1. Thanksgiving is in … — b) November
  2. The first Thanksgiving was in … — b) 1621
  3. On TV people watch … — a) a parade

4. Answer the questions

  1. Who shared the first Thanksgiving dinner? — The Pilgrims and Native Americans.
  2. What do people eat for dessert? — Pumpkin pie.
  3. I am thankful for … — власна відповідь

6. Christmas

1. Find the word in the text

  1. a big sock for presents — stocking
  2. Christmas songs — carols
  3. Santa comes down it — chimney
  4. gifts — presents
  5. to make something beautiful — decorate

2. Fill in the gaps

  1. Children write letters to Santa.
  2. On Christmas Eve they hang stockings.
  3. They leave milk and cookies for Santa.
  4. Presents are under the tree.

3. True or False?

  1. Christmas is the most popular holiday in the USA. — T
  2. Families decorate the tree with lights and toys. — T
  3. Santa Claus comes in through the door. — F
  4. Children find their presents in the evening. — F

4. Answer the questions

  1. What do children leave for Santa? — Milk and cookies.
  2. Where do children find their presents? — Under the tree.
  3. How does your family celebrate Christmas? — власна відповідь
